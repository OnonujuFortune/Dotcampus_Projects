# Title 
`Kwik Market Sales Analysis` 

## SQL-Driven Insights into Customers Behaviour, Sales Performance and Revenue Trends. 

### Intro

*This project explores sales, customers and product data from Kwik Market using SQL to answer key business questions. WOrking within a star-schema MySQL database, payment and delivery preferences, employee sales distribution and revenue trends aimed at surfacing insights that can inform business decisions around inventory, marketing and customer retention.* 

---

### My Task as an Analyst 

*My task as an analyst was to write and execute SQL queries against the kwik market database to answer a set of business analysis questions identifying (top-selling products, revenue by category, customer distribution by stste, payment & delivery method preferences, repeat customer rates, employee sales performance and the impact of customer demographics on purchasing behavior.) This involved working directly with a multi_table schema, applying joins, aggregations and window functions to extract meaningful business insights.* 

---

### Tool Used
`MySQL`

--- 

### Data Preparation 
In the initial data preparation phase, the following task were performed

- Verifying table structure and relationships across the schema using *SHOW TABLES* and information_schema
- Resolving table name/case mismatch when queries returned errors 
- Confirming primary and foreign key relationships between *fact_sales* and each dimensional table.
- Validating column names and data types before writing analysis queries

### Data Exploratory Analysis 

* Which products sell the most, and which generate the most revenue?
* How is revenue distributed across product categories?
* Which state has the highest number of customers?
* Which delivery method do customers prefer most?
* What is the average order value (AOV)?
* Which payment method is most used, and does this vary every month?
* What proportion of customers are repeat buyers?
* How are sales distributed across employees?
* Does gender correlate with purchasing behavior (spend, frequency)? 
* Who are the most popular product category in each state?
* What percentage of total revenue comes from each payment method?
* What is the average number of items per order? 
* How much revenue comes from new vs. existing customers?
